# AI Trading Performance Analyst

Evidence-based trading analytics, journaling, and funded-account (prop firm) rule tracking — in English.
Upload your trade history and see honest numbers: net P&L after costs, expectancy in $ and R, drawdown, session and weekday breakdowns, prop-firm rule meters, strategy checklists, and whether following your rules paid off. Every number shows how many trades it is based on.

**It is not a trading bot.** It never places trades, never gives buy/sell signals, and never promises profits.

- Runs fully in your browser; your data stays on your device. No account, no API key, no cost.
