# Glossary (English)

Starter list. Phase 1 modules add more terms and fuller explanations.

| Term | Simple English |
|---|---|
| Trade | One position from opening to closing. |
| Long (Buy) | You buy, hoping price goes up. |
| Short (Sell) | You sell first, hoping price goes down. |
| Entry price | The price where the trade opened. |
| Exit price | The price where the trade closed. |
| Stop-loss (SL) | A price where the trade closes to limit your loss. |
| Take-profit (TP) | A price where the trade closes to lock in profit. |
| Lot | A unit of trade size. 1 standard lot of EURUSD = 100,000 euros. |
| Pip | A small standard price step (EURUSD: 0.0001). |
| Spread | The gap between buy and sell price; a hidden cost. |
| Commission | A fee the broker charges per trade or per lot. |
| Swap | A fee or credit for holding a trade overnight. |
| P&L | Profit and loss. |
| Realized / unrealized P&L | Closed result vs. floating result of an open trade. |
| Balance | Account money counting only closed trades. |
| Equity | Balance plus the floating P&L of open trades. |
| Drawdown | How far the account fell from its highest point. |
| R-multiple (R) | Result divided by the planned risk. +2R = won twice the risk. |
| Expectancy | Average result per trade over many trades. |
| Win rate | Share of trades that won. |
| Profit factor | Total profit ÷ total loss. Above 1 means profitable. |
| Breakeven | A trade that ended at (almost) zero. |
| Session | A market time period: Asian, London, New York. |
| Prop firm / funded account | A company that lets you trade its money if you follow its rules. |
| Daily loss limit | The most you may lose in one day before the account fails. |
| Max loss limit | The most you may lose in total. |
| Trailing drawdown | A loss limit that moves up as the account makes new highs. |
| Consistency rule | Your best day may not be more than X% of total profit. |
| Read-Back | The app restates what it understood and waits for your confirmation. |
| Sample size | How many trades a number is based on. Fewer trades = less certainty. |
| Paper trading | Practice trading with fake money. |
