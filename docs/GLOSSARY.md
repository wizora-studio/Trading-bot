# Glossary (English + Roman Urdu)

Starter list. Phase 1 modules add more terms and fuller explanations.

| Term | Simple English | Roman Urdu |
|---|---|---|
| Trade | One position from opening to closing. | Ek sauda: kholne se band karne tak. |
| Long (Buy) | You buy, hoping price goes up. | Aap khareedte hain, is umeed par ke qeemat upar jaye gi. |
| Short (Sell) | You sell first, hoping price goes down. | Aap pehle bechte hain, is umeed par ke qeemat neeche aaye gi. |
| Entry price | The price where the trade opened. | Woh qeemat jahan trade khuli. |
| Exit price | The price where the trade closed. | Woh qeemat jahan trade band hui. |
| Stop-loss (SL) | A price where the trade closes to limit your loss. | Woh qeemat jahan trade khud band ho jaye taake nuqsan had mein rahe. |
| Take-profit (TP) | A price where the trade closes to lock in profit. | Woh qeemat jahan trade band ho kar munafa pakka ho jaye. |
| Lot | A unit of trade size. 1 standard lot of EURUSD = 100,000 euros. | Trade ke size ki ikai. EURUSD ka 1 standard lot = 1,00,000 euro. |
| Pip | A small standard price step (EURUSD: 0.0001). | Qeemat ka chhota muqarrar qadam (EURUSD mein 0.0001). |
| Spread | The gap between buy and sell price; a hidden cost. | Khareed aur farokht ki qeemat ka farq; ek chhupa hua kharcha. |
| Commission | A fee the broker charges per trade or per lot. | Broker ki fees, har trade ya har lot par. |
| Swap | A fee or credit for holding a trade overnight. | Raat bhar trade khuli rakhne ki fees ya credit. |
| P&L | Profit and loss. | Munafa aur nuqsan. |
| Realized / unrealized P&L | Closed result vs. floating result of an open trade. | Band trade ka pakka nateeja vs. khuli trade ka abhi badalta nateeja. |
| Balance | Account money counting only closed trades. | Account ki raqam, sirf band trades shamil kar ke. |
| Equity | Balance plus the floating P&L of open trades. | Balance jama khuli trades ka badalta munafa/nuqsan. |
| Drawdown | How far the account fell from its highest point. | Account apni sab se oonchi satah se kitna neeche gira. |
| R-multiple (R) | Result divided by the planned risk. +2R = won twice the risk. | Nateeja ÷ planned risk. +2R matlab risk ka do guna jeeta. |
| Expectancy | Average result per trade over many trades. | Bohat si trades ka ausat nateeja fi trade. |
| Win rate | Share of trades that won. | Kitne fisad trades jeetin. |
| Profit factor | Total profit ÷ total loss. Above 1 means profitable. | Kul munafa ÷ kul nuqsan. 1 se zyada matlab munafa. |
| Breakeven | A trade that ended at (almost) zero. | Woh trade jo taqreeban zero par khatam hui. |
| Session | A market time period: Asian, London, New York. | Market ka waqt: Asian, London, New York. |
| Prop firm / funded account | A company that lets you trade its money if you follow its rules. | Woh company jo apne rules maanne par aap ko apne paise se trade karne deti hai. |
| Daily loss limit | The most you may lose in one day before the account fails. | Ek din mein zyada se zyada nuqsan; is se aage account fail. |
| Max loss limit | The most you may lose in total. | Kul zyada se zyada nuqsan ki had. |
| Trailing drawdown | A loss limit that moves up as the account makes new highs. | Nuqsan ki had jo account ke naye oonche level ke saath upar chalti hai. |
| Consistency rule | Your best day may not be more than X% of total profit. | Aap ka sab se acha din kul munafe ke X% se zyada na ho. |
| Read-Back | The app restates what it understood and waits for your confirmation. | App batati hai ke us ne kya samjha, aur aap ki tasdeeq ka intezar karti hai. |
| Sample size | How many trades a number is based on. Fewer trades = less certainty. | Kitni trades par number mabni hai. Kam trades = kam yaqeen. |
| Paper trading | Practice trading with fake money. | Farzi paison se practice trading. |
